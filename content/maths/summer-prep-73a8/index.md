# GCSE Maths Summer Prep

A short refresher course for students moving from Year 9 into Year 10 Higher Maths.

Designed to rebuild confidence, refresh the key skills and make the start of Year 10 much easier.

## Start here

Work through the lessons in order.

They're designed to build on each other and cover the topics that students most often forget over the summer holidays.

## Note from dad

- Like all skills these need to be practised
- Don't think that you can just learn it once and then get it right 2 years later
- Well some kids can but you probably don't need to be here if that is your kid

Practice tips
- I'm old school
- Get a pad of paper and a few pencils or pens
- Write the equations out for your kids one by one
    - ChatGPT will help you come up with these and explain the solutions
- Always work on paper, not just putting numbers into an iPad. 
- This massively helps reinforce the skills

Habits to get into
- Be neat! Learn to write carefully
- Think before moving to the next iteration of an equation
- You have plenty of paper, use it. Don't cram stuff on.
- Work slowly until you can work quickly.
- 10-15 minutes a day can be a little habit that you can get into. It will destroy 'once a week' homework for results. 
- It can be as little as one or two questions if they are harder because they take time to work through.

And just remember that if your kid gets confident at maths, they will start to enjoy it, or at least not fear the lessons. Being good at something builds confidence and it's a great feeling to sit in a class being able to do the work.
